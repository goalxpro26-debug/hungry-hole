HUNGRY HOLE
Eat Everything

You are Gulp, a little hungry crater loose in the park. Swallow everything smaller than you, grow huge, and out-eat the rival craters before time runs out!

How to play
• Move: drag / move mouse, or WASD / arrow keys.
• Eat anything smaller than your hole — pebbles, mushrooms, cones, benches, cars, Wobblers…
• Eating grows you, so bigger snacks unlock as you grow.
• Rival craters eat too! A bigger rival that touches you costs a life. Outgrow one and eat it for +300. Rivals give you a few seconds at the start of each round before they start hunting.
• Reach the round target score before the timer ends. 20 rounds, 3 lives. Combo eats pay more!
